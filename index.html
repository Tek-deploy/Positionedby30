The first ten independent years

The First Ten Independent Years Set the Trajectory of the Next Forty.

Destiny can either be aligned or delayed. We provide the spiritual grounding, trauma-informed emotional clarity and strategic life counsel young adults (ages 20–30) need to stop drifting, break confusion and build a lasting foundation.

The Truth You Need to Hear:

Confusion can be a natural threshold of growing up, but chronic uncertainty is where spiritual stagnation and doubt take root.

A Special Word to Parents ↗
A Special Word to ParentsTo Parents: Investing in the Trajectory of Your Adult Child Read the message for parentsClose the message

You have prayed over them. You invested in their education. You provided for them through childhood and university. But now, they have entered the most vulnerable window of their entire lives: independent young adulthood.

In their twenties, they are navigating career competition, economic volatility, cultural confusion and spiritual warfare without the daily protection of your roof. Many struggle in silence—afraid of disappointing you, overwhelmed by choices and paralyzed by the fear of failure.

You cannot make their life decisions for them, but you can give them the gift of sanctified, strategic counsel.

Rooted in Biblical Truth

P30 is anchored in Christian doctrine, spiritual discipline and covenant alignment.

Spiritually & Strategically Sound

Led by an AACC-certified counselor trained in spiritual and mental health first aid, and executive positioning.

Practical Competence

We do not offer abstract motivation; we train young adults in real-world professional readiness, financial stewardship and emotional resilience.

Sponsoring your son or daughter for The Alignment Fellowship is an investment that protects the next forty years of their life.
