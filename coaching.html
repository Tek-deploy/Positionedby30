The 5-Pillar Alignment Framework

Most coaching in the world hands you someone else’s formulas: generalized budgeting rules, trending career tracks or hollow motivational speeches on "making an impact."

P30 does not teach formulas; we facilitate alignment.

No amount of strategy, coaching or external discipline can hold if your life is not anchored to your Source. When you are anchored, life itself becomes your classroom. We don’t ask you to copy what worked for another person whose design and assignment are completely different from yours. We bring you back to who you are, how God uniquely wired you and the realities of your current season, so that every decision in money, work, service and future-building flows from true spiritual grounding rather than cultural pressure or panic.

01Identity

Reconnecting and anchoring you back to your Source—because without a spiritual anchor, true life alignment is impossible.

02Money

Moving past generic financial rules to discern the stewardship, realities and financial discipline required for your specific design and season.

03Career

Aligning your natural strengths, cognitive wiring and life experiences into work that authentically expresses who you were created to be.

Structured empirical testing to discover the professional lane that genuinely matches your design.

Closing the readiness and competence gap to step boldly into the domain you are called to occupy.

(Note: The tracks apply strictly to Career—all other life pillars are walked together.)

04Impact

Dismantling vague ambitions to uncover your actual assignment—knowing exactly where, how and to whom you are called to minister real value.

05Legacy

Stopping the pressure to build a monument in your twenties, and learning to seed the daily, compounding obedience that outlives you.
